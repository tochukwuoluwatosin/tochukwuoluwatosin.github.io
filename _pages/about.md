I am a PhD Candidate in Materials Science at Saitama University, Japan, supported by the MEXT (Monbukagakusho) Scholarship. As a chemist by training, my expertise spans Functional Materials & Biosensing, Environmental Health & Natural Products, and Computational & Experimental Modeling.

My research focuses on elucidating the molecular mechanisms of pollutant-induced protein dysfunction, characterizing advanced functional materials, and assessing environmental risk through integrated experimental biophysics and computational chemistry.

On this website, I present my scientific research, list my full publication records, and share updates on my academic activities.

### Research Interests

* Advanced Functional Materials & Biosensing: Synthesis (CQDs, NADES) and multi-scale characterization.
* Computational Biophysics & Modeling: Density Functional Theory (Gaussian, ORCA, Material Studio), Molecular Dynamics (GROMACS, NAMD, LAMMPS), and Molecular Docking (AutoDock).
* Environmental Health & Risk Assessment: Pollutant-protein interaction modeling, micro/nanoplastics toxicity, exposure science, and predictive ML models.
* Spectroscopic & Analytical Characterization: Fluorescence, UV-Vis, FTIR, Raman, NMR, LC-MS, DLS, TEM, SEM, XRD, and XPS.

### Research Vision

My research goal is to establish a comprehensive, predictive framework for assessing the environmental and human health risks posed by emerging contaminants. By integrating experimental biophysics, computational chemistry, and machine learning, I aim to elucidate the molecular mechanisms of pollutant-induced protein dysfunction and develop robust exposure science models. This research generates critically needed data for environmental risk assessment, public health policy, and the design of safer, more sustainable materials.
