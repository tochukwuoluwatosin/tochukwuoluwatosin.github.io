I am a **PhD Candidate in Materials Science** at **Saitama University**, Japan, supported by the prestigious **MEXT (Monbukagakusho) Scholarship**. 

My research lies at the intersection of **Materials Science, Environmental Biophysics, and Applied Physics**. I specialize in characterizing advanced materials, investigating biophysical interactions, and developing sustainable functional material systems for environmental and biomedical applications.

---

## Research Focus & Expertise

* **Advanced Functional Materials:** Synthesis, structural characterization, and property optimization.
* **Environmental Biophysics:** Interfacial phenomena, bio-material interactions, and sustainable material solutions.
* **Spectroscopic & Analytical Techniques:** Microscopic characterization, elemental analysis, and material degradation studies.
