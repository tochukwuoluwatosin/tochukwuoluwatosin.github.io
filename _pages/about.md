I am a **Materials Scientist** completing my PhD at **Saitama University, Japan** (expected completion: September 18, 2026) supported by the Japanese Government **MEXT Doctoral Scholarship**[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span).

My research lies at the intersection of **experimental biophysics, computational chemistry, and environmental exposure science**[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span). I specialize in synthesizing green nanomaterials (CQDs, NADES), conducting advanced physical characterization, and running multiscale molecular dynamics simulations (DFT, MD, docking) to elucidate pollutant-protein interactions[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Vision

My primary research objective is to establish predictive frameworks for assessing environmental and health risks from emerging contaminants like micro/nanoplastics and atmospheric pollutants[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span). Combining spectroscopy with *in silico* molecular simulations allows us to understand pollutant-induced protein dysfunction at an atomic level[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
